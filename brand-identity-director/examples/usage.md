# Example requests and expected behavior

## discover

“Use $brand-identity-director in discover mode for a local repair cooperative. We have a name, two customer interviews, and an old wordmark. Define positioning and a practical identity brief.”

Expected: inspect supplied assets; distinguish two interviews from a representative sample; identify audience tasks, equity, and critical contexts; produce intake/strategy/evidence log. Do not demand a contract or invent competitor research.

## explore

“Explore an identity for POBEMA, a pre-order operations platform for small producers. Avoid generic rocket or shopping-cart symbols. Give two genuinely different routes with header/avatar studies.”

Expected: provisional brief if needed; two credible organizing ideas and system behaviors; comparable context studies; no palette-only differences or claimed testing. An illustrative route could express shared scheduling through rhythm, another clarity through typographic hierarchy. Neither is asserted to be the final brand solution.

## design

“Develop selected territory B from the attached strategy. Include logo variants, type and color roles, and a multilingual product-label/web-header study.”

Expected: use selected direction; inspect script coverage and smallest-use constraints; preserve masters; specify print questions; create actual artifacts where tooling permits. Do not reopen rejected routes without cause.

## critique

“Critique this SVG and label mockup against the brief. Prioritize issues; do not redesign.”

Expected: validate supported SVG profile; inspect visuals; distinguish unsupported features from malformed/unsafe content; scorecard with evidence and retests. No unsolicited new identity or legal clearance.

## refine

“Refine the attached mark to preserve its counters at 24 px. Keep the idea and type treatment.”

Expected: preserve baseline and equity; adjust gaps or micro variant; compare at 24 px under matching conditions; report what improved and any remaining limitation. Do not redesign the entire system.

## deliver

“Package the approved identity for web and packaging. SVG and PNG now; printer-ready PDF only after the converter provides a dieline/profile.”

Expected: verified available exports/guidelines/manifest; explicit provisional print status; font licensing notes; no fake PDF/AI files, no inferred website publishing, no trademark clearance claim.

## full

“Direct the full identity for refillable household cleaners. Use attached audience research, bilingual copy, converter dieline, and budget. Include packaging, campaign assets, and digital guidelines.”

Expected: stage-appropriate artifacts from strategy to handoff; decisions recorded; application constraints and rights reviewed; actual-size packaging proof and digital accessibility checks identified as performed or pending. No forced concept count.

## Worked reasoning fragment (synthetic, no audience study)

SUPPLIED: a repair cooperative wants customers to understand repair as accessible local help.
REPORT-H: R1 §3.3 suggests pairing strategic terms to generate form ideas.
HYPOTHESIS: an open joining seam could suggest reconnection without industrial severity.
Alternative reading: chain link or infinity sign; compare nearest category marks.
PRACTICE: show a typographic route alongside the seam route to avoid overcommitting to symbolism.
Proposed test: unprompted service interpretation and brand identification on a shop sign and small receipt. No participant results are claimed.
