# Verbal identity

R1 §1.3 and R2 §5 provide the boundary-word and short-statement exercises (`REPORT-H`). Adapt the count and character limit to the task; compulsory repetition can make copy unnatural.

Define message hierarchy: what the brand does, who benefits, distinctive promise, reasons to believe, and next action. Check every performance, health, sustainability, or comparative claim against supplied substantiation and current local requirements before public use. Leave unverified claims flagged in drafts.

Describe voice as stable principles with examples, then tone as context-dependent. A brand can be friendly in onboarding and sober during an outage. Useful axes include direct ↔ expansive, formal ↔ conversational, and restrained ↔ expressive, with concrete boundaries. Avoid assigning emotions mechanically to line weights.

Draft a headline, a short introduction, a useful product description, a service error, and one packaging or social message. For each, show a preferred version and a plausible misuse. Select words to favor and words to avoid because they change meaning, not to meet an arbitrary quota. Record spelling, capitalization, pronunciation, abbreviations, locale variants, and naming hierarchy.

Pair copy with visual studies at the same level of fidelity. Test whether layout hierarchy supports the intended message and whether tone remains usable at small sizes and in translated text. Do not claim a mismatch “destroys trust”; describe the specific tension and gather responses. Preserve room for longer translations, right-to-left layouts, and script-specific typographic conventions.

Naming is a separate workstream when requested: generate candidates from strategy, check linguistic and category ambiguity, document available screening scope, and route legal questions to a qualified professional. A domain search or available username establishes neither trademark availability nor ownership. Read [rights](rights-and-trademark.md) before finalizing names.

Output a voice table, message ladder, example copy, prohibited unsupported claims, and localization notes in the [guidelines](../templates/brand-guidelines.md). Distinguish approved statements from proposals.
