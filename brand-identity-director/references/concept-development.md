# Concept development and selection

R1 §3.3 and §5.3 contribute keyword pairing and narrative presentation (`REPORT-H`). Exact-three presentations and stretch concepts as psychological anchors are not requirements. No decoy concept is needed.

Start from approved or provisional strategy, critical applications, equity to retain, and anti-goals. Generate meaningful words, actions, tensions, and material behaviors. Pair terms when it reveals a useful relationship; do not force every value into a pictogram. Expand with typographic ideas, spatial logic, product behavior, editorial direction, and non-literal visual languages.

## Build distinct territories

Choose a concept count suited to scope and time, often a few credible routes. Compare their organizing ideas, silhouette/composition, type behavior, imagery, layout, and application rules. If replacing one palette or typeface makes two routes identical, they are likely variations within one territory. A shared strategic anchor is acceptable; artificial difference is not the objective.

Use a territory matrix before polishing: idea → strategic relevance → form language → system behavior → key application → likely confusion → test. Sketch broadly in low fidelity, eliminate redundant routes, then develop comparable presentations. Maintain an exploration log so the model does not recycle a discarded shape under a new story.

## Show the work fairly

Present flat marks, one-color studies, type specimens, and at least the application's most demanding context. Keep fidelity, copy, scale, and mockup quality comparable across options. A dramatic mockup can disguise a weak asset. Rationale follows intent → mechanism → expected effect → evidence/test, without invented audience endorsement.

Recommend a route using agreed criteria and disclosed tradeoffs. Keep stakeholder preference separate from measured performance. Ask for a selection only when a material branch needs the decision; otherwise make a provisional recommendation and continue reversible work within scope. Record direction changes and do not silently treat a recommendation as client approval.

## Refinement loop

Use [scorecard](../templates/critique-scorecard.md) to identify the top issues. Change the variable that addresses the finding, preserve a baseline, and compare the same contexts. Do not combine incompatible concepts because each contains a favored detail without checking resulting coherence. Stop when acceptance criteria are met or when further improvement needs unavailable evidence or a decision; report that limit honestly.
