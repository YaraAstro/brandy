# Rights, provenance, and trademark caveats

This skill can organize preliminary similarity and provenance checks. It cannot provide trademark clearance, determine registrability, guarantee originality, or promise exclusive rights in an AI-assisted output. State that limit in concept and handoff documents without burying it in fine print.

## Preliminary design screening

Record proposed name/mark/version, countries/markets, goods/services, languages, relevant competitors, search date, sources, query variants, and results. Compare appearance, wording/sound, and overall impression within the actual commercial context. Include related categories where relevant. Retain closest neighbors and unresolved issues. “No match found in these sources” is the maximum scoped observation; “available,” “safe to register,” and “cleared” are not supported conclusions.

The [USPTO's comprehensive search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks) explains that U.S. screening extends beyond a federal database and includes common-law use. That is U.S.-specific guidance, not a global legal procedure. A logo board, domain search, business registration, image similarity score, or empty search result cannot replace jurisdiction-specific professional advice. Have the client obtain qualified trademark counsel or an appropriate specialist before relying on clearance, filing, or major launch investment. Do not file or purchase anything as an inferred action.

## Asset rights ledger

For every font, photograph, illustration, icon, mockup, and generated element, record source/creator, license or terms version/date, intended uses, modification/embedding/redistribution permissions, restrictions, proof of acquisition, and open questions. Do not assume a web font license permits desktop logo work or client redistribution, or that outlined/customized letters have no remaining obligations. Check actual terms with the provider or qualified advisor when uncertain.

Keep references and inspiration separate from deliverable assets. Do not trace a distinctive competitor element and call it original. Record generative tool/model/date, inputs, edits, applicable service terms, and any source restrictions when AI imagery is used. Model output alone is not an exclusive-rights certificate. Client ownership/transfer terms must come from the actual agreement; do not invent legal clauses or claim a transfer occurred.

## Delivery wording

“Preliminary design similarity review: [scope/date/sources]. Unresolved concerns: [items]. No trademark clearance or legal opinion is provided; jurisdiction-specific review remains [owner/status]. Font and image use is subject to the listed licenses.”

For the two archived NotebookLM reports, the user supplied the text but public redistribution rights were not established. Keep source provenance attached and review permissions before publicly sharing this package. Source-report claims are evidence inputs, not legal or scientific authority.
